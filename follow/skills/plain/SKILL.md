# plain - the last message, said again in plain words

The user did not follow the message the assistant just sent.
Not part of it - any of it.
Say that same message again, simpler, in the language they are writing in, and open with a line of context, because they may have lost even what it was about.

Four things the instinct gets wrong:

- **It's not a summary.** Three points in, three points out. Fewer words, same substance.
- **Simpler, not softer.** A detail that costs money, deletes something, or only works under a condition stays in.
- **Use the words already in play, drop the ones that failed.** A term that didn't land won't land inside a longer sentence - say what it means instead. One word per thing, even when it repeats.
- **No preface.** No apology, no "let me put that differently", and don't ask which part they missed.
